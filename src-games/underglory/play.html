Underglory

Lead the morning glory up to the arch. It will not get there on its own.

  • Trellis — pick it up, then drag on the paper to build a rail. The vine finds supports and climbs them. Rails stop at walls. Your stock is the fill in the icon.
  • Plant food — pick it up, then click the ground. Everything growing nearby speeds up until it is used up.
  • Remove a rail — press and hold a rail you have already built. It comes back into stock in full.
  • Prune — press and hold any stem or leaf. What you cut feeds the rest: pruning returns rail and food, and pushes the freed growth out to the furthest tip. Every flower you prune is worth points.

There is no time limit — the clock only decides how much of the time bonus is left. A course runs three vines at most: once all three are growing, a prune stops making new ones and speeds up whichever vine is nearest the arch instead. The dotted line ahead of each tip is where it is currently headed.

Nothing here can be lost. If a vine wedges itself it will find another way on its own, and any level can be skipped from Settings.
